# Tampa Signs and Stickers branding

The black and white transparent PNG logos supplied in the conversation are used directly, with no lettering redraw or font redistribution. The three-page branding PDF informed the teal/black/orange direction. Its page 1 business cards and page 3 teal identity variations are references, not claims of a single approved web color specification.

Web palette (adapted for UI): near-black #0B0F10; primary teal #16B2B4; darker button/text teal #087F81; orange accent #F28A3B. White text uses the darker teal rather than the brightest teal on primary buttons. Full accessibility conformance has not been audited.

Assets: app/static/brand/tampa-black.png and tampa-white.png. The white asset has transparency and is intended for dark backgrounds; it is not empty. Logo art stays owned by its original rights holder. No proprietary font files are included.
